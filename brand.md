# Brand — Archis

Status: active

Archis is a precise, composed optical tracking instrument. The interface uses a
graphite base, cool neutral surfaces, cyan for active controls and measured optical
data, green for verified lock, amber for acquisition or degraded operation, and red
only for faults. Typography is compact and highly legible; tabular numerals are used
for telemetry. Copy is direct, operational, and honest about measured versus pending
results.

The product avoids decorative aerospace imagery inside the console. The camera feed,
tracking overlays, metrics, and run evidence are the primary visual material.
